1. About this policy

This policy applies to the Skillyte mobile app and the Skillyte website. It explains how we handle personal data under the laws that apply to us, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules. By creating an account, you (or your parent or guardian, if you are under 18) agree to the practices described here.

2. Who we are

The Skillyte app and website are run by Skillyte. We decide why and how your personal data is used, which makes us the data fiduciary (also called the data controller). Our contact details are in section 14.

3. Children and parents

Skillyte is designed for young people, and many of our learners are under 18. We take extra care with their information.

  • For learners under 18, we ask for the consent of a parent or lawful guardian before we create an account or use personal data. [Describe how this consent is collected and checked.]
  • We do not show advertising in Skillyte, and we do not use children's personal data for targeted advertising.
  • We do not track or monitor children's behaviour to profile them for commercial purposes. We use analytics and crash reports only to keep Skillyte working and to improve lessons. [Confirm analytics settings for learners under 18.]
  • A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their child's information, or withdraw consent, at any time.

If you believe a child has joined Skillyte without a parent or guardian's consent, please contact us and we will act promptly.

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, email address if you give one, preferred language, and your age or date of birth.
  • Location details: [village, district and state, if collected].

  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system, app version, language settings, IP address, push notification token and crash logs.

5. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you join programs in your language.
  • To save your progress and show your results.
  • To send verification codes, service messages and notifications.
  • To answer your questions and give you support.
  • To keep Skillyte secure and prevent misuse.
  • To fix errors and improve the app and its lessons.
  • To share your progress with your partner organisation, [if you joined through one].
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We use your personal data on the basis of your consent, or your parent or guardian's consent if you are under 18, and for other purposes the law allows, such as meeting legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Where your data is stored

Our main servers are in India, on Amazon Web Services (Mumbai region). Some of the providers in section 6 may handle limited information in other countries. We choose providers carefully, share only what is needed, and require them to protect it.

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct or update your information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Make a complaint and get it resolved.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or are unable to.

For learners under 18, a parent or guardian can make these requests. Write to support@skillyte.com. We will reply within [timeframe] and resolve complaints within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied after using our complaint process, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
