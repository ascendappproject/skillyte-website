Support

How can we help?

Questions about using Skillyte, signing in, your account or your child's data? Start with the answers below, or write to us directly.

Contact us

EMAIL
support@skillyte.com

Please never share your password or verification code with anyone, including Skillyte staff.

I did not receive my verification code

Check that your mobile number was entered correctly and that you have network coverage. Wait one minute, then ask for a new code. If it still does not arrive, contact us and tell us the number you registered with.

I cannot sign in, or I lost access to my account

Contact us using the phone number or email address registered on your account. We will check that it is really you before we change anything.

How do I change my language?

Open the app and go to [Profile > Language], then choose the language you want to learn in.

Something in the app is not working

Update Skillyte to the latest version and restart it. If the problem continues, write to us with your phone model, your Android or iOS version, the app version, what happened, and a screenshot if you can.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from inside the app, or from our website even if the app is no longer installed.

Go to Delete my account

I am a parent or guardian. How do I ask about my child's data?

Write to us at support@skillyte.com. We can help you see, correct or delete your child's information, or withdraw your consent.

Read our Privacy Policy

I run an organisation and want to use Skillyte with my learners

We would like to hear from you. Write to support@skillyte.com and tell us about your organisation and the young people you work with.

Have a privacy concern or complaint?

Write to our Grievance Officer at grievance@skillyte.com. Our Privacy Policy explains how to reach us and what to expect.
