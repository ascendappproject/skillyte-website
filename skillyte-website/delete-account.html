Delete my account

Delete your Skillyte account.

This page is for the Skillyte app, run by Skillyte. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it, even if the app is no longer installed on your phone.

Deleting your account is permanent.

Your profile, progress and results cannot be recovered afterwards. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account or your data.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

This is the fastest way if you can still open Skillyte.

1

Open the Skillyte app and sign in.

2

Go to [Profile > Settings].

3

Tap [Delete account].

Option 2: Request deletion here

Use this if you cannot open the app or have uninstalled it. If the account belongs to a learner under 18, a parent or guardian should make this request.

Before we delete anything, we will check that the details you give match your account.

You can also email support@skillyte.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Skillyte account".

What happens next

1

We check it is you

We check that your request matches your account details within [number] days.

2

We delete your data

We delete your account and personal data within [30] days, and remove it from backups within [90] days.

3

We confirm

We tell you when it is done, using the contact details you gave us in your request.

What we delete

  • Your name, mobile number, email address, language and age details
  • Your learning progress, quiz results and [certificates]
  • Your parent or guardian's details, if you are under 18
  • Your push notification token and device identifiers
  • Messages you sent to our support team, [after the retention period in the Privacy Policy]

Want to remove only some data?

You do not have to close your account. Write to support@skillyte.com and tell us which information you would like removed or corrected.
